Why these are free

Each one is the honest, useful half of a larger paid kit. If the free version is all you need, take it and go — it's complete on its own, and there's no email wall in front of it. If it helps and you want the full system, there's a link inside each tool. That's the whole arrangement.

These are organizational and educational tools. They are not legal, medical, financial, psychological, or educational-placement advice, and they do not evaluate you, your family member, or your child. Only a qualified professional who knows your actual situation can do that.

Processes, eligibility, and timelines vary by state and district, and they change. These tools deliberately do not print deadlines. Confirm anything time-sensitive with a professional or the relevant office where you live.
