Monday moves (do these even if we never talk)

  1. Pick one live letter type. Not a policy memo. A letter that actually leaves the building.
  2. Name the system of record for the balance, the dates, and the itemization. If two systems disagree, you do not have a letter problem yet. You have a truth problem.
  3. Require a word-level diff against that record before send. Tone review is not a substitute.
  4. Give compliance a view that is not the revenue dashboard. They should be able to stop a send without owning the quota.
  5. Count refuses. A shop that never refuses is grading costume only.
