STEP 1 OF 2

Which tests are in your report?

Check every one you can find. They're usually in a results table near the back. Don't worry about the ones you don't recognize — pick what matches.

STEP 2 OF 2

Type in the numbers

Enter only the scores you can find — blanks are fine. Most reports list a Standard Score (a number near 100). Some list Scaled Scores (near 10) or percentiles. Use the little dropdown to match whatever your report shows.

Can't tell which kind you have? If the number is between 40 and 160, it's almost always a Standard Score. If it's between 1 and 19, it's a Scaled Score.
