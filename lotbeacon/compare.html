On the roadmap, not in the product

These are what their pages claim and we do not ship. Marked here so the table does not pretend.

  • Measured show rate and kept appointments. Podium and STELLA print stories. Ours stays modeled until a store counts it.
  • A deal packet: thread, unit, trade, two slots, who sent it. Hand to the desk, then to finance. No credit application, and no RouteOne or DealerTrack submit, until a person is on it.
  • SMS next to Messenger. Same rule: a person hits Send.
  • A confirmed slot written to Xtime or the CRM. Impel, Podium, and STELLA already claim the write. We will not write it without the rep.
  • OEM co-op. Impel claims GM iMR. STELLA claims FordDirect. We will not put a badge on the page until a program exists.
