Vs the field

We leave features out on purpose.

Other desks will auto-reply, auto-book, or quote a number the store did not approve. That is not a missing feature here. It is the product. Sources are the policies, not a competitor scoreboard.

No autonomous send

A person still hits Send. Meta’s Messenger policy gives a standard window (usually 24 hours) and a Human Agent tag for a person responding manually. Where the law requires it, an automated chat must say it is automated. We do not ship a bot that talks as the store.

Source: Messenger Platform policy — standard messaging window and Human Agent tag; automated-experience disclosure.

No invented discount, payment, or hold

Drafts may only quote live inventory. A price ask escalates. The FTC treats deceptive advertising as illegal. The CFPB watches auto advertising and financing claims. A tool that types “$2,500 off today” without a person is the liability.

Sources: FTC Advertising FAQs · CFPB auto loans

No off-platform scrape, no personal cell

Paste-in or the Page inbox. Store number only. Unwanted texts are an FCC problem, not a growth channel.

Source: FCC — stop unwanted robocalls and texts

Not Beakon. Not a BDC replacement.

Beakon is lot GPS. LotBeacon is a Messenger sales copilot. We do not sell headcount replacement. Meta Inbox is free. The paid layer is intent, the queue, the firewall, and the escalate.
